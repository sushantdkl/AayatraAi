# Aayatra AI Sales Engine — Market and ICP Research

**Research date:** 2026-10-01  
**Scope:** Initial desk research and testable hypotheses for Nepal. This is not a market-size forecast.

## D. Market/ICP Map

### Evidence baseline

- Nepal's Ministry of Culture, Tourism and Civil Aviation publishes [Nepal Tourism Statistics 2024](https://tourism.gov.np/content/83/nepal-tourism-statistics-2024/), including a table of registered tourist-standard hotels. This validates hospitality as a real, enumerable sector; it does not establish Aayatra's serviceable market or prove a digital gap.
- The Office of Company Registrar operates a [public company data portal](https://company.ocr.gov.np/) and reports hundreds of thousands of registered companies. Registration does not prove that a company is active, reachable, suitable, or legally contactable.
- Nepal's Inland Revenue Department provides a [PAN search](https://ird.gov.np/pan-search/). It is suitable for targeted verification where permitted, not assumed bulk lead harvesting.
- Nepal has a [Privacy Act, 2075](https://lawcommission.gov.np/content/12261/the-privacy-act-2075/) and an [Electronic Transactions Act, 2063](https://lawcommission.gov.np/content/13397/). Counsel must decide lawful basis, notice, retention, direct-marketing, and channel rules before production acquisition or outreach.

The master prompt's claims about weak sites, manual operations, and social-commerce growth are therefore hypotheses. The first campaigns must measure their prevalence and commercial value instead of encoding them as facts.

## ICP qualification model

Fit should combine evidence across four axes rather than equating “no website” with a good lead:

1. **Commercial reality:** active business, reachable business contact, apparent decision-maker path, location and operating status.
2. **Operational complexity:** volume, branches, staff, rooms/tables/SKUs/variants, suppliers, revenue centers, reconciliation needs.
3. **Problem evidence:** observed digital gap plus a confirmed or carefully labeled inferred operational pain.
4. **Solution feasibility:** verified Aayatra capability, deployability, budget class, implementation capacity, and no disqualifying custom dependency.

Keep lead fit, digital maturity, and buying intent separate. Public observations may inform fit; only prospect behavior should drive buying intent.

## Initial experiments

Use small, manually reviewed cohorts. Suggested numbers are experiment sizes, not sending quotas.

| Experiment | Cohort | Measurement | Success evidence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Hospitality digital gap | 100 properties across Kathmandu, Pokhara, Chitwan and one secondary market | Site/direct-booking presence, restaurant/event signals, verified contactability | Prevalence by signal and qualified-opportunity rate |
| Combined hospitality | 50 properties with lodging + dining/event evidence | Confirmed fragmented workflow through discovery call | Meeting/proposal value versus hotel-only cohort |
| Social cosmetics | 50 manually/legally sourced business profiles | Structured catalogue/checkout, variants, public business contact, activity | Positive reply and confirmed inventory pain |
| Kawaii/accessories | 50 businesses | SKU/variant proxy, DM-order language, ecommerce maturity | Qualified deal size and ERP/ecommerce interest |
| Restaurant operations | 100 restaurants stratified by size/location | KOT, stock, booking/site signals; call validation | Problem-confirmation and demo conversion |
| Salon | 50 multi-staff salons | Booking/CRM/site maturity | Booking/retention pain and willingness to change |

Each experiment needs a dated sampling frame, source policy, review rubric, contact policy, negative outcomes, and funnel report. Stop or change a segment if opt-outs/complaints are high, contact evidence is poor, delivery capacity is missing, or opportunities do not justify acquisition cost.

## Research data to collect

- Denominator: businesses evaluated, not only imported “leads.”
- Source and sampling method.
- Evidence-backed signal prevalence and confidence.
- Duplicate, inactive, wrong-category, and unreachable rates.
- Qualification, reply, positive reply, meeting, proposal, deposit, and revenue rates.
- Time and provider/AI cost per qualified opportunity and per won deal.
- Outcome by location, segment, bundle, source, and evidence quality.
- Human corrections to classification and product routing.

## Claims that remain unproven

- The size of Aayatra's serviceable or obtainable market.
- That Nepal businesses in any named segment generally have the described problems.
- That social engagement predicts budget or purchase intent.
- That combined bundles convert better or yield better margins.
- That a specific Aayatra product currently supports any capability listed in the prompt.
- Any conversion, revenue, CAC, or purchase-probability estimate.

## Commercial validation gate

Move an ICP from “hypothesis” to “validated” only after a documented sample shows repeatable problem confirmation, verified product fit, acceptable contact/complaint quality, delivery capacity, and economically useful progression to proposals or deposits. Record confidence intervals or raw cohort counts; do not present tiny-sample percentages as durable forecasts.
