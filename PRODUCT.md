# Aayatra AI Sales Engine

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

The master prompt prefers Next.js, TypeScript, React, PostgreSQL, Redis and BullMQ. The first implementation uses Next.js, TypeScript and PostgreSQL. Redis and BullMQ are introduced when a tested background job is needed.

## Users

Aayatra's owner, sales team, product approvers and delivery staff. The primary daily user is a sales operator deciding whom to contact next, recording evidence and moving opportunities through an accountable pipeline.

## Product Purpose

Identify qualified businesses, recommend appropriate Aayatra offers from verified knowledge, organize conversations and proposals, and measure revenue from real customers.

## Positioning

An internal sales operating system tied to Aayatra's restaurant, hotel, retail, salon, website, ecommerce and automation portfolio. Recommendations are grounded in approved product facts and source evidence.

## Operating Context

Manual and imported business leads, activities, contact preferences, qualification, opportunity stages, tasks, customer conversations and product approval. External social discovery and outreach require separate channel and source approval.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The first usable increment is a manual CRM with organization scoped users, businesses, contacts, leads, opportunities, activity and stage history.
- A product capability can be customer facing only after a product owner verifies and publishes it.
- The system keeps lead fit, digital maturity and buying intent distinct.
- The demo website link will be supplied later by the user. Do not invent it.
- No product feature, price, customer, performance claim or delivery date is verified by the current repository.

## Brand Commitments

Use Aayatra Enterprises and the product name Aayatra AI Sales Engine. The product brief calls for a premium, dense B2B interface with quick scanning and clear AI versus human actions.

## Evidence on Hand

The master prompt and six Phase 0 research and architecture documents in the project root. Product repositories, demonstrations, release notes, pricing sheets, identity assets and the demo website link have not been supplied.

## Product Principles

1. Show the next important human action first.
2. Preserve evidence and history for every claim and stage change.
3. Keep external sending and commercial commitments under explicit policy.
4. Make manual operations useful before adding automation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard accessible controls, readable dense tables, responsive layouts, clear focus states and status text that does not rely on color alone are required by the master prompt.
